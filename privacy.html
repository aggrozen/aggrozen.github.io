Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

AggroZen Uploads is a private tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, granted through Google sign-in, it can upload videos and set their titles, descriptions and thumbnails on the owner's YouTube channel, and read that channel's basic details to confirm it is signed in to the right channel. It does not read or collect data about anyone else.

Storage and sharing

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
